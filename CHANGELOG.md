# Pixelito changelog

Every update to Pixelito, newest first. Installed copies show these notes after updating.

## 1.2.1 · Tidier panels and full corners

*Released 2026-11-06*

- Tool options now sit at the top of the right panel, above the colors, and only show when the tool has settings.
- Drawing and erasing keep every pixel you touch, so corners fill in completely. Corner trimming is now an option: Smooth lines (trims corner pixels).
- Phones: tools are grouped into Brush, Shapes and Select tabs, the top bar takes two rows instead of three, and the timeline buttons are neatly arranged.
- The Pick tool is now called Color Picker.

## 1.2.0 · Brush sizes

*Released 2026-11-06*

- Brush size slider from 1 to 100 pixels, plus a box to type an exact size.
- One-tap size buttons from 1×1 to 10×10, easy with a finger or Apple Pencil.
- The eraser has its own size, and Pixelito remembers both.

## 1.1.0 · Move freely, choose your folders, and stay up to date

*Released 2026-11-06*

- Sharper checkerboard: its squares now line up with your pixels.
- New Hand tool (H) to move the board anywhere. You can also drag the empty space around the board.
- The zoom buttons and keys keep the board in place. In the View panel, choose whether the mouse wheel zooms toward the pointer or around the board.
- Windows: choose where your work is saved, in the installer and any time under File > Save folders.
- Pixelito now tells you when an update is ready, and shows what changed after you update.

## 1.0.0 · First release

*Released 2026-11-06*

- Pixelito by FluffyAmigo Studio: a pixel-art and animation editor for Windows, iPad, Android and the web.
- Golden welcome screen, works offline, Apple Pencil support, and sprite sheet export for Unity.
