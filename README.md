# Pixelito

**Little app, big sprites.** A free pixel-art and animation editor by FluffyAmigo Studio.

**Open it:** https://fluffyamigotv.github.io/pixelito/

- Works in any modern browser on iPad, Android tablets and phones, and PCs, with Apple Pencil and stylus support.
- Installs to your home screen and works offline. No account, no ads.
- Your projects stay on your own device.
- Exports sprite sheets with JSON for Unity, plus tilesets, GIFs and PNGs.

**Install on iPad or iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then *Add to Home Screen*.
**Install on Android:** open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then *Install app*.

A Windows program is also available from FluffyAmigo Studio.
